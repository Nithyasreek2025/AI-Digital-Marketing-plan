# AI Tools Used in Digital Marketing

## 1. ChatGPT
Purpose:
- Generate marketing ideas.
- Create social media captions.
- Write promotional messages.
- Prepare content calendars.

## 2. Canva AI
Purpose:
- Design attractive posters.
- Create advertisements.
- Prepare social media graphics.

## 3. Google Analytics
Purpose:
- Track website visitors.
- Understand customer behaviour.
- Measure marketing performance.

## 4. Meta Business Suite
Purpose:
- Manage Instagram and Facebook pages.
- Schedule social media posts.
- Monitor audience engagement.

## 5. Conclusion
These AI and digital marketing tools help local businesses improve their online presence, create content and understand their customers.

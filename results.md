# Expected Results and Evaluation

## 1. Project Evaluation
This project demonstrates an AI-supported digital marketing plan for Sweet Treats Bakery.

## 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Social media reach
- Likes, comments and shares
- Customer enquiries
- Website visits
- Number of customer orders

## 3. Illustrative 30-Day Targets
- Publish 12 social media posts.
- Create 4 promotional videos.
- Improve customer engagement.
- Respond to customer enquiries within one business day.
- Review marketing performance every week.

Note: These are proposed targets, not actual measured results.

## 4. Evaluation Method
The business can monitor weekly social media insights, customer enquiries and website visits to understand marketing performance.

## 5. Limitations
No real business account or live analytics data was connected. Therefore, the results are expected outcomes rather than measured results.

## 6. Conclusion
AI tools can help local businesses create content, plan marketing campaigns and understand customer engagement. Human review is important to ensure accuracy and quality.

# AI Digital Marketing Plan for a Local Business

## Project Overview
This project focuses on creating an AI-based digital marketing plan for a local business.

## Objectives
- Improve online business visibility.
- Reach more customers.
- Increase social media engagement.
- Promote products using AI tools.

## AI Marketing Strategies
1. Social Media Marketing
2. Content Creation using AI
3. Customer Targeting
4. Digital Advertising
5. Performance Analysis

## Tools Used
- ChatGPT
- Canva
- Instagram
- Google Analytics

## Expected Outcome
To improve the online presence and growth of a local business using Artificial Intelligence.

## Conclusion
AI helps local businesses improve their digital marketing strategies and reach more customers.

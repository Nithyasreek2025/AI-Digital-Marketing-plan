# AI Digital Marketing Campaign Strategy

## 1. Campaign Overview
Campaign Name: Sweet Treats Bakery Digital Promotion

Business Type: Local Bakery

Campaign Goal: Increase brand awareness, attract new customers and improve sales using AI-powered digital marketing.

## 2. Target Audience
- Students
- Families
- Working Professionals
- Local Residents

## 3. Social Media Campaign

### Instagram
- Post attractive bakery product photos.
- Share short promotional reels.
- Announce special offers.
- Share customer feedback.

### Facebook
- Publish product updates.
- Share festival offers.
- Connect with local customers.

### WhatsApp Business
- Share product catalogues.
- Send promotional messages with customer permission.
- Answer customer enquiries.

## 4. AI Tools Used

### ChatGPT
Used for creating captions, promotional messages and marketing ideas.

### Canva AI
Used for designing posters and social media advertisements.

### Google Analytics
Used to understand website traffic and customer engagement.

## 5. Sample Advertisement

"Fresh Cakes, Delicious Cookies and Tasty Pastries!

Visit Sweet Treats Bakery today and enjoy freshly baked happiness.

Special Offer: Get 10% off on selected bakery products!"

## 6. Campaign Schedule

| Week | Activity |
| Week 1 | Create social media pages |
| Week 2 | Publish product advertisements |
| Week 3 | Promote special offers |
| Week 4 | Review campaign performance |

## 7. Performance Measurement
- Number of social media followers.
- Post likes and comments.
- Customer enquiries.
- Website visits.
- Sales enquiries and orders.

## 8. Expected Outcomes
- Better online visibility.
- Increased customer engagement.
- Improved brand awareness.
- More potential customers.
- Better marketing performance.

## 9. Conclusion
AI-based digital marketing helps Sweet Treats Bakery reach more customers through social media, creative content and effective advertising strategies.
